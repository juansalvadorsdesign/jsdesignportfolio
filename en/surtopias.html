MANY HANDS

PROJECT 03

Surtopías is a platform for sociocultural action that proposes a creative journey through pedagogy to redefine the relationship between art and today’s society.

Through collective, cross-disciplinary ways of working, stereotypes are challenged and new settings for art criticism are explored, understanding and practising collaboration as an essential tool for social change.

  1. Role

    Design and organisation

  2. Period

    2025

SKILLS ACQUIRED

MULTIDISCIPLINARY COLLABORATION

Perspectives and methodologies from very diverse fields of knowledge were brought together, enriching the project’s approach and fostering a truly cross-disciplinary working ecosystem.

DESIGN LEADERSHIP

Leadership of the graphic design team was taken on, guiding the creative process and ensuring the visual identity accurately reflected the critical, social and transformative character of the movement.

MANAGING INSTITUTIONAL COMMUNICATIONS

Formal relationships were established and managed with leading cultural institutions such as the Centre Pompidou, adapting tone and presentation to the standards those organisations require.

LARGE-SCALE EVENT PLANNING

The visual and spatial logistics needed to host a large number of attendees were coordinated, ensuring that both the visitor experience and the project’s message came across optimally during the gatherings.
